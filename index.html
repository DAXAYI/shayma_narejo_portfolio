Mobile App Case Study

Home Interior Decor App

A real mobile-product UI project designed around home-interior discovery. The interface includes launch/authentication screens and a content-rich home experience for browsing categories, room inspiration, and furniture collections.

ProblemCreate a visually coherent mobile experience that helps users discover interior inspiration and browse home-decor products without overwhelming the screen.
SolutionA dark, warm-toned interface with focused navigation, visual category controls, room imagery, product cards, search access, and clear login/sign-up entry points.
What I DesignedLaunch screens, authentication entry, home screen hierarchy, category navigation, image-led room sections, product collection cards, and bottom navigation.
